Outwit Vex, the Cinder Imp. Spend mana, summon sigil creatures, and break the enemy's 30 life before yours falls.

HOW TO PLAY
Each turn you draw a card and gain mana (1 → 10).
Click a card in your hand to play it. Spells need a target — click the spell, then its target.
Creatures fight at the end of every round: each one strikes the creature opposite it, or the hero if the slot is empty.
Elements matter: ◆ Ember beats ▲ Grove, ▲ Grove beats ● Tide, ● Tide beats ◆ Ember — a winning strike deals +1 damage. ✦ Storm fears nothing and bows to nothing.
